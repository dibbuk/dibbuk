# M&A IT integration: two businesses transitioned in 48 hours

[Русская версия](ma-integration-48-hours.ru.md) · [Back to profile](../README.md)

## Project context

Following an acquisition, two businesses previously owned by US companies had to be integrated into the corporate IT environment. The key risk was losing access to technology data and infrastructure during the ownership and IT transition, with production and business continuity at stake.

## My role

**IT Project Manager · Rosatom Composite Division · 2016–2025**

My responsibilities across the M&A workstream included IT due diligence, integration planning, coordination of internal IT and external parties, risk assessment, transition planning and execution.

## What I did

- I led the IT transition of **two acquired businesses simultaneously**.
- Organized the move into the corporate domain and IT environment after the transaction.
- I managed dependencies and mitigated transition risks around access to systems, technology data and infrastructure.
- I coordinated stakeholders and technical teams and completed the transition without interrupting production or business processes.
- I developed a repeatable IT integration plan for acquired assets, drawing on my experience integrating **six assets** into the division's digital ecosystem.

## Outcome

- I transitioned both businesses into the corporate domain within **48 hours after the deal**.
- Production and business processes continued **without downtime**.
- The experience contributed to a standard IT M&A integration plan used as a repeatable basis for subsequent integrations.

## What this case demonstrates

Time-critical integration under business-continuity constraints; IT due diligence and transition planning; risk and dependency management; coordination across acquired businesses and corporate technology teams; and the ability to turn a one-off high-pressure integration into a repeatable approach.
