# MES localization and production digitalization

[Русская версия](mes-royal-canin.ru.md) · [Back to profile](../README.md)

## Project context

A Russian production facility needed to replace a foreign MES solution and move critical production and laboratory workflows away from manual work and legacy tools affected by vendor restrictions.

The assignment covered the full delivery lifecycle: from business case, requirements and solution alignment through development, integration, testing, go-live and transition to support.

## My role and delivery scope

**Project Manager · Ventra IT Solutions (Mars / Royal Canin) · February 2025 – July 2026**

- Project budget: **RUB 80M+**.
- Cross-functional delivery environment: **60+ participants** in a weak matrix structure.
- Coordinated **two external development teams** and internal IT, architecture, information security, finance, legal, operations, quality, R&D, laboratories and warehouse stakeholders.
- Business and systems analysts were in my direct reporting line.
- Worked in a hybrid Waterfall / Agile delivery model.

## What I led

1. **Business case and requirements:** benefits and ROI calculations, AS-IS / TO-BE analysis, functional and non-functional requirements, business requirements and technical specifications.
2. **Solution alignment:** architecture reviews, integration scope, dependencies and technical decisions with internal teams and vendors.
3. **Commercial and delivery management:** RFI / RFP, vendor selection criteria, SOW and contract work, CAPEX / OPEX considerations, WBS, schedule, resources, budget, risks and critical path.
4. **Execution and change control:** backlog formation and prioritization, progress metrics, blocker removal, escalations, stakeholder communications and scope-change assessment.
5. **Quality and transition:** test planning, SIT / UAT coordination, business acceptance, cutover, go/no-go, go-live, training, documentation and handover to support.

## Solution delivered

- Custom MES core and **14 modules**, covering **40+ processes**.
- Integrations with **Navision ERP, 1C:WMS and ELMA365**.
- Production and equipment integration using **Kafka, SCADA, OPC UA and Historian**.
- Real-time Control Point dashboards for production KPIs, including TRS / OEE, ATS, productivity, energy consumption, incidents, CAPA and audit results.
- A Continuous Improvement Process roadmap for further MES development and migration of remaining production processes.

## Measurable outcomes

- **20+ critical production and laboratory processes** moved to alternative corporate solutions, reducing exposure to vendor withdrawal and production downtime risks.
- **100+ processes** analyzed; critical processes prioritized for migration and automation.
- Data discrepancies reduced by **10%** through automation and removal of manual data entry in the implemented scope.
- ROI figures recorded for implemented process scope: **Green — 21%; Blue — 28%**.

## What this case demonstrates

End-to-end ownership of a complex enterprise technology implementation; management of a large cross-functional, weak-matrix delivery environment; business-to-technology translation; vendor and commercial management; integration of production and enterprise systems; and transition from project delivery to continuous improvement.
